🎁 How to Play?

1
Create or join a room
Share the link or code with your rival. Choose your role and nickname.
2
Vote game rules
Each player picks regions, types, and restrictions. Consensus merges both choices.
3
Confirm when ready!
Both players confirm. The system filters and pre-loads the Pokémon pool.
4
Turn-based drafting (10s)
Two Pokémon appear on each turn. Pick one before the timer runs out.
5
Export to Pokémon Showdown
Once completed, copy your team directly into Pokémon Showdown for battle!
